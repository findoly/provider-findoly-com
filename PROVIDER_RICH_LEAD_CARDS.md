# Rich Lead Card UI

This release refines provider lead cards without changing lead, pricing, CRM-sync, wallet, or outcome logic.

- Lead titles are sentence-cased for display only; stored data is unchanged.
- Marketplace cards show preferred timing, lead age, provider interest, and current result in compact coloured insight tiles.
- Unlocked lead cards show customer, preferred timing, outcome, activity, and unlock/action timing in compact coloured insight tiles.
- Intent, outcome, overdue, and activity states use restrained semantic colour.
- Card height and empty whitespace are reduced across laptop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
