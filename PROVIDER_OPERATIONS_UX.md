# Findoly Provider Operations UX

This release keeps the existing provider workflows and CRM synchronization while organizing the portal around daily provider tasks.

## Main experience

- Command-centre dashboard with marketplace, lead, follow-up, confirmation and pending-outcome counts.
- Priority queue for outcomes, follow-ups, new opportunities and confirmed business.
- Quick marketplace filters for urgent-priority, fresh, low-competition and unconfirmed leads.
- Quick My Leads filters for action required, follow-up, confirmed and not confirmed.
- Lead cards with decision-ready context and direct Call/WhatsApp actions after unlock.
- Lead-detail quick action rail for customer contact and outcome updates.
- Responsive desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.
- One consistent lightweight Professional Blue appearance across the full portal.

No pricing, authentication, radius, wallet, Razorpay, outcome, reminder or CRM synchronization rules were removed.
