# Provider Conversion and Mobile Navigation Update

## Mobile navigation

- The mobile drawer uses one explicit open state.
- The drawer is always above its overlay.
- The overlay blocks page content only while the drawer is open.
- Menu links, close, outside tap, Escape, resize, and browser restore are handled.
- Focus is moved into the drawer and trapped while it is open.
- Page scrolling is restored after closing.

## Marketplace conversion UI

- Marketplace leads use full-width decision cards on laptop and desktop.
- The unlock cost and primary action remain visible in a dedicated action panel.
- The action panel explains what becomes available after unlocking.
- Lead intent, distance, timing, age, unlock activity, and confirmation status remain transparent.

## My Leads

- Customer contact and outcome actions remain visible in the action panel.
- Call, WhatsApp, and Update Outcome are prioritised.
- Overdue outcomes remain highlighted.

No provider pricing, CRM synchronization, radius matching, wallet, Razorpay, reminder, or outcome business rules were changed.
