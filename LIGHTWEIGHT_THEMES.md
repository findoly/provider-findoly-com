# Provider portal full-page themes

The provider portal includes three lightweight appearance choices. The selected theme is stored in the provider's browser and does not change provider data, workflows, permissions, pricing, or CRM synchronization.

Each theme now applies consistently to the complete portal:

- top header and account menu;
- desktop sidebar and mobile navigation;
- dashboard, Marketplace, My Leads, lead details, profile and Credits pages;
- cards, tables, filters, forms, buttons, empty states and dialogs;
- body and heading typography, font weight, spacing and corner treatment.

Themes:

- Network Blue — structured business workspace with Segoe-style typography
- Social Sky — friendly rounded workspace with Helvetica-style typography
- Calm Chat — quiet green communication style (default)
- Creative Mint — crisp marketplace style with Avenir-style typography
- Talent Green — focused professional workspace
- Trade Orange — compact seller workspace with direct typography
- Local Coral — friendly local-services workspace
- Care Teal — calm service and healthcare workspace
- Clear Neutral — minimal neutral workspace
- Warm Sand — soft warm workspace with serif headings

All themes remain light. Success, warning, rejection, invalid and error colours retain the same meaning across every theme.
