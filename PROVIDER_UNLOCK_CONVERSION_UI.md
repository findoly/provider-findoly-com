# Provider Unlock Conversion UI

This release keeps the existing provider portal business logic and refines the lead experience around one goal: helping providers identify relevant opportunities and unlock them confidently.

## Marketplace changes

- Full-width marketplace summary that cannot compress into a side column.
- Clear result, available-credit, and active-filter information.
- Conversion-focused opportunity cards showing priority, location, distance, timing, age, provider unlock activity, current confirmation signal, and unlock cost.
- Recommended badge for urgent-priority opportunities with low provider activity.
- Prominent **View & unlock lead** action with the required credit amount.
- Clear explanation that customer contact and full lead access become available after unlock.

## Lead details

- Locked leads include a prominent customer-contact unlock banner.
- Providers with sufficient credits can unlock directly.
- Providers without sufficient credits are taken to the existing credit/direct-payment options.

## Responsive behavior

- Two opportunity columns on suitable desktop/laptop widths.
- One column on smaller laptop, tablet, and mobile screens.
- Search, filters, stats, and actions reflow without clipped or letter-by-letter text.
- Mobile navigation and the bottom action bar remain above page content and tappable.
