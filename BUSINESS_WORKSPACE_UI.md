# Findoly Business Workspace UI

This provider release uses a neutral business-workspace design intended for healthcare, retail, professional services, home services, agencies and other provider categories.

## Main areas

- Action-led dashboard with available credits, matching leads, unlocked leads and pending outcomes.
- Two-column marketplace opportunity cards on desktop and single-column cards on smaller screens.
- Advanced filters for category, location, priority, confirmation, activity, dates and credit cost.
- Customer and outcome-first unlocked lead workflow.
- Responsive navigation with a mobile bottom bar.
- CRM-managed provider service location remains read-only.

All existing provider authentication, wallet, Razorpay, lead-radius, outcome-reminder and CRM synchronization behavior is retained.
